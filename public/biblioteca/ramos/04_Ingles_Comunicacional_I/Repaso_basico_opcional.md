## 5. Technical abstract

**Dificultad:** Intermedio  
**Tema:** Writing
**Tiempo:** Sin límite  
**Puntaje:** Práctica

Write an 80-100 word abstract about controlling VOC emissions at a Chilean chemical terminal. Include the problem, two technologies and one conclusion.

<details>
<summary>Pista</summary>

Use a four-part flow: context, aim, technologies, conclusion.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Solución</summary>

Model answer: This report examines the control of volatile organic compound emissions at a chemical terminal in Quintero, Chile. VOCs may escape during storage and product transfer, creating environmental and occupational risks. The study considers vapor recovery units, which capture valuable product, and regenerative thermal oxidizers, which destroy residual vapors at high temperature. It also highlights the importance of sealed systems, monitoring, and preventive maintenance. The analysis concludes that effective emission control requires both reliable equipment and disciplined operation, supported by trained personnel and transparent environmental monitoring.

</details>

Greet, state the topic, explain why it matters, and preview the talk.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Solución</summary>

Model answer: Good morning. Today I will present our case study on air decontamination in the chemical industry, focusing on VOC control at the Oxiquim terminal in Quintero. Then, I will compare vapor recovery and thermal oxidation technologies. Finally, I will describe how mechanical engineers can improve system design, monitoring, and maintenance.

## 7. Answering a technical question

**Dificultad:** Intermedio  
**Tema:** Speaking
**Tiempo:** Sin límite  
**Puntaje:** Práctica

Answer in English: 'Why is maintenance as important as installing the pollution-control equipment?'

<details>
<summary>Pista</summary>

Mention seals, sensors, efficiency and operation outside the design range.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Solución</summary>

Model answer: Maintenance is essential because even a well-designed system loses efficiency when seals deteriorate, sensors drift, or ducts become damaged. Preventive inspections help detect leaks and keep the equipment within its design range. Without maintenance, the company may have higher emissions, energy consumption, safety risks, and unplanned shutdowns.

</details>

## 10. Engineering conditionals

**Dificultad:** Avanzado  
**Tema:** Grammar
**Tiempo:** Sin límite  
**Puntaje:** Práctica

Complete with the correct forms: (a) If the sensor ___ (fail), the alarm ___ (not activate). (b) If the company ___ (maintain) the seals regularly, emissions would be lower. (c) If the VRU had captured the vapor, less product ___ (be) lost.

<details>
<summary>Pista</summary>

Use first, second and third conditional patterns.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Solución</summary>

(a) If the sensor fails, the alarm will not activate. (b) If the company maintained the seals regularly, emissions would be lower. (c) If the VRU had captured the vapor, less product would have been lost.

</details>
