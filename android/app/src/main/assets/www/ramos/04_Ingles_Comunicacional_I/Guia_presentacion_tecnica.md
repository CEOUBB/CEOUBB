# Guía de presentación técnica en Inglés

## Estructura breve

1. Greeting and topic.
2. Why the problem matters.
3. Objective and scope.
4. Main technical findings.
5. Engineering contribution.
6. Conclusion and questions.

## Frases útiles

- `Today I will present...`
- `The main objective of this study is...`
- `The system consists of...`
- `The results indicate that...`
- `From a mechanical engineering perspective...`
- `To conclude...`
- `Thank you for your attention. I am happy to answer questions.`
